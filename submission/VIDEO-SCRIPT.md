## Optional narration

ListCheck helps operations teams compare two recurring business lists without uploading records to a cloud service.

Start with two CSV or Excel files. Their rows can be reordered, and their columns can have different names.

Map the fields you want to compare. Choose a unique ID for exact matching, and use names or addresses to find candidates when IDs are missing or different.

ListCheck uses character-level TF-IDF similarity to suggest possible counterparts. A similarity score is not a probability: you review the original fields and decide.

Confirming a match reserves that record and updates the field differences. You can undo the decision.

For exact matches, inspect changes side by side. Here, River View has a different owner and status.

The prototype also generates CSV review reports and reusable field settings. Unreviewed records remain explicitly labeled.
